FAQ

Remapping: frequently asked questions

Is it safe? What about the warranty? What are torque, BHP and Nm? Find the answers here.

What exactly do you do?

Our technicians connect to the ECU, the vehicle's "brain", with a laptop through the diagnostic socket and read the original engine management file, which is then sent to our file department and optimised specifically for your vehicle. The technician then programs the new optimised Quantum file into your vehicle, and at that point you can experience the incredible results for yourself. On some vehicles this cannot be done through the diagnostic socket: in that case the ECU is removed and the work is carried out directly on it, with a chip change or direct boot-mode programming. The end result is exactly the same; only the process differs. Depending on the vehicle, the remap usually takes less than an hour. You can choose to visit one of our dealers or our head office in Iver or, depending on the area, we can come to you at home or at the office.

Is it safe for the vehicle?

Our Quantum remapping process improves and optimises engine power within safe limits, never beyond them. More power also means fewer gear changes. Manufacturers have to build in a tolerance for engine parts, to allow for wear and production variations and to keep warranty claims to a minimum during the warranty period, which is normally 3 years.

Most race and rally engines are pushed for every last bhp and are therefore more prone to failure, whereas ordinary car, van and lorry engines are detuned and sit well away from the likelihood of multiple failures. Every engine is different in how far it can be pushed with a remap, but a good remap from a quality tuner will never come close to the failure limits of the components.

You will find that the best companies in the market have all developed similar performance gains. Be wary of tuners who claim significantly more: either they are lying, hoping the average customer will not be able to tell the difference, or the engine is being pushed too hard and will quickly become unreliable.

Why don't manufacturers do this?

Manufacturers generally sell a vehicle that has to cover various markets, with different tax and emissions regulations, different climates with extremes of heat, cold and humidity, different fuel qualities, different operating altitudes and so on. A manufacturer has to take all these factors into account and make considerable compromises in how the vehicle operates.

Sales and marketing also have a big influence on the way an engine is mapped. If you look at the engine specifications in the manufacturer's brochure, the torque curve rises, then flattens into a straight line and then drops. Obviously that is not how an engine breathes: this flat torque line is a sales and marketing requirement. Why? It is about having better torque figures than the competition and about the tax laws of the local market.

What guarantee do you offer?

A professionally tuned engine will not cause technical failures, provided it is well maintained and regularly serviced. In the extremely unlikely event of a problem, we stand 100% behind the quality of our product, should any fault be found to have been caused by the upgrade.

Can you explain what remapping is?

If you want to tune your diesel vehicle while keeping the safety functions and the durability of the engine, transmission and ESP, the only way is to make changes within the existing engine control system. That is exactly what we offer with a Quantum remap. Our tuning process keeps total control over all parameters at all times and does not interfere with the car's safety functions by fooling the system. Besides the obvious benefits in power, torque and fuel economy, a Quantum remap gives you the following advantages:

  • The Quantum remap is installed within the hour, usually without even lifting the bonnet
  • Nothing needs to be connected to the vehicle after tuning to get the benefits
  • No boxes or loose wires in the engine bay
  • Torque comes in softly and smoothly
  • Torque is regulated/limited in the lower gears (to avoid damage to the transmission)
  • Boost pressure and airflow are optimised
  • The original settings can be reinstalled if necessary
  • The on-board computer shows the correct fuel consumption
  • The electronic stability programme (ESP) remains fully functional
  • Torque reduction when changing gear (for comfort and durability) is retained
  • Regeneration (emptying) of the diesel particulate filter (DPF) is optimal
  • Correct calculation of exhaust gas temperature
  • Correct calculation of the service interval
  • The diagnostic system is retained
  • No increase in NOx, HC, CO, CO2 or soot emissions compared with the original at MOT and service time
Do you offer a trial period?

We are very confident that your Quantum remap will improve your driving experience and save you a considerable amount on fuel. But don't just take our word for it: try the upgrade for 30 days and, if you are not satisfied with the results, we will remove it and refund you in full.

What about insurance?

Please note that it is your responsibility to inform your insurance company of any modification to your vehicle. We are finding that more and more insurers do not penalise their customers for a fuel-saving upgrade and recognise our economy remaps. We can provide a certificate of conformity if required.

Do you keep a copy of the original tuning file?

Yes, in a secure archive, in case you ever need to return your vehicle to its original condition.

Does this affect the manufacturer's warranty?

Some manufacturer diagnostic tools may detect a remap. That said, many forward-thinking manufacturers turn a blind eye to a quality remapping service, knowing that a properly upgraded vehicle can only benefit them. The official line of most manufacturers is that if a modification causes a problem with the vehicle, the affected part will not be covered by the warranty; this does not mean a unilateral cancellation of the entire warranty.

How does remapping improve the vehicle?

A full Quantum engine remap changes the engine's fuelling, turbo and torque characteristics by controlling fuel pressure, the quantity of fuel injected, the maximum visible smoke limit, turbo pressure and the torque curve.

A Quantum remap offers a fully controllable modification of the engine's characteristics, while still operating with full engine feedback control and within all the limits preset by the engine's sensors.

What do diesel tuning boxes do?

We do not fit tuning boxes, which normally just interfere with the engine management signals to trick the injectors into adding more fuel. Our service is a custom remap of your ECU, specific to your vehicle.

Tuning boxes work differently from a remap. They are based on the principle that more diesel fuel = more diesel power. The theory is correct and tuning boxes do increase power, but they cannot control the amount of black smoke (the maximum visible smoke limit) coming out of the exhaust.

Too much smoke is undesirable, both environmentally and visually. More importantly, black smoke is unburnt diesel fuel that clings to the combustion chamber, with deposits coming straight out of the exhaust pipe: it is therefore wasted fuel that has contributed nothing to the increase in power. The results are higher emissions, a higher thermal load on the engine (i.e. exhaust gas temperature) and, often, faults such as "jerking". It should be clear what this means for your engine.

A modern engine has complex control systems that work together to keep everything running perfectly. A diesel tuning box manipulates certain signals to increase the injection time or the fuel pressure. Unfortunately, this manipulation is done entirely without the engine control system knowing about it. This means that a diesel tuning box fools the vehicle's engine control system, and as a consequence a large number of values and control data will be incorrect, with consequences such as:

  • The on-board computer shows improved fuel consumption, but the actual amount left in the tank is lower, because fuel is being injected without any of the sensors detecting the extra quantity entering the cylinder.
  • The electronic stability programme (ESP) is disrupted because of the incorrect torque value.
  • Boxes and loose wiring in the engine bay.
  • Automatic gearboxes receive an incorrect torque value.
  • Torque reduction when changing gear (for comfort and durability) is not retained.
  • Regeneration (emptying) of the particulate filter will not be optimal.
  • The exhaust gas temperature calculation is thrown off.
  • The service interval calculation is thrown off.
  • The diagnostic system is disrupted.
What is torque?

Torque is the rotational force created in an engine by its rotating parts; power is the measure of how quickly that torque is delivered. Power and torque work together, since power is calculated from torque.

For the average buyer, torque should not be a deciding factor unless you know you will be towing or carrying heavy loads. This is where high-torque vehicles perform well: when towing a trailer or running at maximum load capacity. A high-torque vehicle will find it easier to accelerate and hold its speed in any situation, but especially with heavy loads.

High-performance cars can also produce a great deal of torque, which helps enormously with acceleration. That feeling of your eyes being sucked into the back of your head in a fast car when you plant your right foot is an example of torque in abundance.

What is BHP (brake horsepower)?

Brake horsepower (BHP) is the measure of an engine's power before the losses caused by the gearbox, alternator, differential, water pump and other auxiliary components such as the power steering pump, the muffled exhaust system, etc. The term "brake" refers to a device that was used to load an engine and hold it at a desired rotational speed. During testing, the output torque and rotational speed were measured to determine the brake horsepower. Power was originally measured and calculated using the "indicator" (an invention of James Watt from the late 18th century), and later by means of a De Prony brake connected to the engine's output shaft.

More recently, an engine dynamometer is used instead of a De Prony brake. Although the power delivered to the driving wheels is less than that available at the crankshaft, a chassis dynamometer (rolling road) gives an indication of the engine's "real" power after the losses in the drivetrain and gearbox. This provides a reasonably accurate indication of how a vehicle's engine will behave once on the road.

What is BDM (Background Debug Mode)?

The Background Debug Mode (BDM) interface is an electronic interface that allows embedded systems to be debugged. In the tuning world, this means that to program an ECU in BDM mode, the ECU has to be removed from the vehicle and programmed on a workbench.

The interface allows a host to manage and query a target. Specialised hardware is required in the target device, whereas no special hardware is needed in the host: a simple bidirectional I/O pin is sufficient.

What is Nm (newton metre)?

A newton metre is a unit of torque (also called "moment") in the SI system. Its symbol is Nm or N·m. One newton metre, sometimes hyphenated as newton-metre, is equal to the torque resulting from a force of one newton applied perpendicularly to a moment arm one metre long.
